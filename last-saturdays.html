Before you come

Good to know

Do I have to be Greek or an HBCU alum?

No. Divine Nine chapters and HBCU alumni are who we built it around, but the door is open to everyone who wants to be in the room.

How do I reserve a chapter table?

Grab the Chapter Table ticket below, or email us with your chapter and how many seats you need and we'll hold it.

What comes with a VIP section?

A reserved section, bottle service, priority entry and a host for your group. Sections seat six to ten — email us if you're bringing more.

What does the Designated Driver ticket include?

Everything a regular ticket includes except alcohol, plus free mocktails, soft drinks and coffee all day, a gold DD wristband, and priority parking.

Can I sell at the event?

Yes — Black-owned vendors are welcome. Book a vendor table below or email us for the setup details and load-in time.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets transfer to any future Last Saturdays. For a refund, email us at least 72 hours before the event.

Is there a dress code?

Grown and together. Rep your letters and your school colors — that's the whole point.
