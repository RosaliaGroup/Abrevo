A standing monthly appointment

Homecoming lasts a weekend.
This lasts all year.

Last Saturdays takes the culture that HBCU alumni and Divine Nine members already organize their year around, and gives it a permanent monthly home in the New Jersey and New York corridor.

Every last Saturday of the month, 11 AM to 5 PM, at Life After Restaurant & Lounge in Edgewater. Twelve themed events built on the real rhythm of the HBCU calendar — season openers, homecoming, the classics, founders' season, spring fest, commencement, conclave.

A daytime, seated, family-of-choice brunch with programming. Not a nightclub promotion. Not a one-off pop-up.

Before you come

Good to know

Do I have to be Greek or an HBCU alum?

No. Divine Nine chapters and HBCU alumni are who the series is built around, but the room is open to anyone who wants to be in it.

What does a ticket include?

Seated brunch service, the full six hours of programming, the marketplace, and the day's stage moments. Bar service is separate unless your tier says otherwise.

What does the Designated Driver ticket include?

Everything general admission includes, minus alcohol — plus free mocktails, soft drinks and coffee all day, a gold DD wristband, and priority parking.

Can I reserve a table for my chapter?

Yes. Email us with your chapter and how many seats you need and we'll hold a section together.

Can I sell at the event?

Marketplace Partner tables are reserved for Black-owned retail, beauty, apparel, food and wellness brands — three to five per event. It includes a table during service, a mention in the printed program, one social feature, and two general admission tickets.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets transfer to any future date in the season. For a refund, email us at least 72 hours before the event.

Is there a dress code?

Grown and together. Rep your letters and your school colors — that's the point.
