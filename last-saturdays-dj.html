Before you come

Good to know

Do I have to be Greek or an HBCU alum?

No. Divine Nine chapters and HBCU alumni are who the series is built around, but the room is open to professionals, entrepreneurs, creatives, educators, athletes and community builders alike.

What exactly does my ticket cover?

General Admission includes a reserved seat, the live podcast hour, a brunch entrée from a curated menu, and one signature cocktail or mimosa — with tax and a 20% gratuity already built into the price. VIP Front Row adds priority entry, a reserved front section, a premium entrée, two signature cocktails, mimosa carafe service, a shared starter and a gift bag.

What's the Early Bird ticket?

The first thirty tickets released each month go at the Early Bird price. When they're gone, general admission opens.

How does the chauffeur service work?

It's booked separately from your ticket and quoted per request, because the price depends on the vehicle, how many people are riding, where you're going and how long you need a chauffeur. Send the request form above and we'll come back with a price — nothing is booked until you confirm.

How do I book a table for my chapter or a birthday?

Reserved tables seat six or eight and come with a bottle-service tier. Book one directly above, or email us if your group is larger.

Can I sell at the event?

Marketplace Partner tables are reserved for Black-owned retail, beauty, apparel, food and wellness brands — a handful per event. It includes a table during service, a mention in the printed program, one social feature, and two general admission tickets.

Is there a dress code?

Themed to the month. For The Kickoff Classic: classic HBCU style, school colors and letters, Greek paraphernalia, luxury tailgate.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets transfer to any future date in the season. For a refund, email us at least 72 hours before the event.
